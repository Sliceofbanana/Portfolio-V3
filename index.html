About me

I build production-grade web systems that generate revenue and solve real business problems.

With 2+ years of experience delivering full-stack solutions, I've helped businesses launch booking platforms, sales dashboards, training portals and e-commerce systems that scale.

From February to July 2026 I led development of ATMS for Agila Tax Management Services — an enterprise platform with 140+ screens that runs the firm's sales, accounting, tax compliance, HR and payroll, plus a client portal, a training academy and an operations portal around it.

I take projects from concept to deployment — architecture decisions, database design, frontend development and production deployment. Through Project SNGDNN, I help businesses, churches and organizations build complete digital foundations: branding, modern websites and scalable systems designed for long-term growth.

My approach: business outcomes first, technology second. Whether you need a WordPress site in two weeks or a custom application with complex business logic, I deliver systems that work reliably in production.

  • Experience with startups and established businesses
  • Direct communication — no project managers in between
