Made for Agila Tax Management Services. The brand belongs to its owner; shown here only as a sample of my work.

Logo

Eagle mark

An eagle's head, its feathers swept into stripes inside a blue circle, beside a widely spaced AGILA and the full name. Alternative versions are used only when they suit the space, always with clear space and the proportions kept.

Colour

Tap a swatch to copy

The colours stand for faith, passion and love, used together as one visual language. Royal blue leads, a pale blue supports it, white keeps it open.

Type

Two families · Google Fonts

Montserrat for headings, Open Sans for reading. Sub-headings get wide letter spacing for a premium, boutique feel.
