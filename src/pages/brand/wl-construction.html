Logo

Roof-shaped W

A W whose right stroke folds into a roofline over a small four-pane window, so it reads as a builder at a glance. It sits above the name in gold, with the line "Building with Integrity, Delivering with Pride."

Gold on white is only 1.8:1, so it's kept for the large logo. Small text in gold always sits on navy (7.9:1).

Colour

Tap a swatch to copy

Deep Navy, Construction Gold, Soft White and Charcoal Gray: professionalism, quality, cleanliness and durability. Navy leads, gold highlights.

Type
