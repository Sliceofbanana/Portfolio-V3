About me

Business outcomes first,
technology second.

I'm a full-stack developer from Cebu. I like the unglamorous part: working out how a business actually runs, then building software that fits it.

I started as an intern at Boombox in March 2024. Since then I've built booking sites, a print shop's order system, a training academy and sales dashboards for businesses across the Philippines.

From February to July 2026 I led development of ATMS for Agila Tax Management Services — an enterprise platform with 146 screens that runs the firm's sales, accounting, tax compliance, HR and payroll, plus a client portal, a training academy and an operations portal around it.

I do graphics too. I design for SFC Redemptorist, and I have been on YCOM Cebu's documentation team since 2023 and CFC's as well. At CTU I was creative head of our graduating class council and creative officer for ICpEP.SE. That's why I care how a product looks and reads, not just whether it works — and why clients like WL Construction come to me for branding as well as websites. See some of my graphics →

I take projects from concept to deployment — architecture decisions, database design, frontend development and production deployment.

Whether you need a WordPress site in two weeks or a custom application with complex business logic, I deliver systems that work reliably in production.

  • Full project ownership — from wireframes to production
  • Experience with startups and established businesses
  • Direct communication — no project managers in between
