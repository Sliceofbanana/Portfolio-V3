FAQ

Good to know
before we start.

How does payment work?

Websites and systems are paid in three parts: 30% to start, 35% when the design is approved, and 35% when the build is done, before it goes live. Smaller jobs under ₱10,000 are 35% to start and 65% on delivery. Bank transfer works, and you get an invoice for every payment.

How long does a project take?

A business website usually takes a few weeks. Portals and custom systems take around two to three months. You'll get the timeline in your quotation, with a date for each milestone.

How many revisions do I get?

Two major and three minor rounds of changes for each milestone. New features or changes outside the agreed scope are quoted separately before any work starts, so there are no surprise charges.

Can I see the work while it's being built?

Yes. You get a private preview link early on and can click through it as features land. I also send a short progress update every week.

Who pays for the domain and hosting?

You do, and they stay in your name. I can buy and set them up for you, and you reimburse the cost plus a small setup fee.

Who owns the website when it's done?

You do. Once the final payment is in, the site, its content and your accounts are handed over to you, along with a how-to guide and a training session for your team.

What happens after launch?

Fixes are free for 2 months after launch (3 months for bigger platforms). After that you can pick a maintenance plan or pay per hour only when you need something.

Can you rush my project?

Often, yes. A shorter timeline adds 25% and urgent priority adds 50%, depending on what's already on my schedule.
