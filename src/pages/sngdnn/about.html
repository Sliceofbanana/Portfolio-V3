Most recently, Genesis led development of ATMS for Agila Tax Management Services (Feb – Jul 2026): an enterprise platform with 140+ screens covering sales, accounting, tax compliance, HR and payroll, plus a client portal and a training academy. That is the kind of system SNGDNN now builds for its clients.
