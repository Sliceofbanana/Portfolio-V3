Logo

PS mark + wordmark

Three blocks that read as an inverted P and an S, for Project SNGDNN, next to a squared-off wordmark. SNGDNN comes from the Cebuano "Sinugdanan", the beginning. The middle step is the only Hot piece; everything else takes the text colour.

Clear space

Keep one letter-height clear on every side. Nothing else sits inside the dashed line.

24px · compact, nav40px · full

Minimum size

Full logo 40px tall on screen, 12mm in print; below that drop "PROJECT" and use the compact version, down to 16px. For favicons use the mark on its own.

  • Stretch it
  • Recolour it
  • Put it on orange
  • Tilt it

Don't

Stretch, recolour, rotate or outline it, or put the full-colour logo on the brand orange, where the middle step disappears. On orange use solid black.

Voice

How it sounds

First person, plain words, short sentences. It should read like a message from the person who will actually build the thing.

Say

  • "I'll get back to you within 24 hours."
  • "Your site loads fast and shows up on Google."
  • "Here's what it costs and what you get."

Not

  • "Our team will reach out at its earliest convenience."
  • "Leveraging cutting-edge synergies for digital transformation."
  • "Contact us for a custom quote."
