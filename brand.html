The SNGDNN
brand kit.

Project SNGDNN is the studio name I build and design under. This is the kit behind genesisjr.com: the wordmark, the colours, the type and the small rules that keep it looking like one thing.

← Back to design

Logo

Pixel wordmark

A blocky, arcade-style wordmark with a hot red-to-orange fill and a dark red drop. It is the only mark, so it stays as it is: no icon, no tagline lock-up, no redraws.

Clear space

Keep one letter-height clear on every side. Nothing else sits inside the dashed line.

18px22px · nav

Minimum size

18px tall on screen, 6mm in print. Below that the pixels blur together.

  • Stretch it
  • Recolour it
  • Put it on orange
  • Tilt it

Don't

Stretch, recolour, rotate, outline or place it on the brand orange, where the fill disappears.

Colour

Tap a swatch to copy

Mostly dark, one loud colour. Asphalt carries the page, Hot does the shouting, and the gradient is saved for the things you should press.

Inside the wordmark

The logo's own fill runs Flare to Ember, top to bottom. Use these only when recreating the mark, never as interface colours.

Voice

How it sounds

First person, plain words, short sentences. It should read like a message from the person who will actually build the thing.

Say

  • "I'll get back to you within 24 hours."
  • "Your site loads fast and shows up on Google."
  • "Here's what it costs and what you get."

Not

  • "Our team will reach out at its earliest convenience."
  • "Leveraging cutting-edge synergies for digital transformation."
  • "Contact us for a custom quote."
