Client: WL Construction

Giving a Cebu builder a brand as solid as its work

Brand Identity • Graphic Design • Company Profile • Social Media

Project Overview

WL Construction is a Cebu builder working on residential, commercial and institutional developments. As design consultant through Project SNGDNN, I created its brand guidelines and the company materials built from them.

The Challenge

A builder earns work on trust, and clients form an opinion from everything they see before a project starts. WL Construction needed one clear identity that could carry across its logo, company profile, social media and business cards, and present services from residential builds to interior fit-outs in a consistent way.

My Role

Design consultant through Project SNGDNN, in 2026:

  • Designing the logo and its variations
  • Setting the colours, typography, pattern and imagery direction
  • Putting it all together as brand guidelines
  • Designing the company profile
  • Applying the brand to social posts and business cards

The Process

  • Start with the mark. The logo is a W shaped like a roof over a small window, so it reads as a builder at a glance.
  • Build the system. Navy and construction gold, Inter for type, a repeating W pattern and a direction for imagery.
  • Document the rules. The brand guidelines show how each element should be used.
  • Apply it everywhere. The profile, social posts and business cards were all built from that system.

The Solution

Brand identity

  • Primary logo and variations
  • Works on gold, navy and white backgrounds

Visual system

  • Colour palette and typography
  • Repeating W pattern and imagery direction

Company profile

  • Six service areas, each with its own photo
  • Project showcases of completed work

Everyday materials

  • Social media posts, including hiring
  • Business cards

Results & Learnings

  • One consistent identity across the logo, profile, social posts and business cards.
  • A company profile ready to present to prospective clients.
  • A reusable system that future materials can follow.

What I learned: a logo is only the start. The real value is in the rules that keep every later piece looking like the same company.
