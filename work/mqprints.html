Client: MQ Printing Services

Making printing in Cebu easier to find and order

Website Design & Development • Customer Portal • Online Ordering & Quotes

Project Overview

MQ Printing Services is a 24-hour print shop in Cebu City. I designed and built their website and the online system behind it, so customers can send in print jobs at any hour and the team can run those jobs from one place.

The Challenge

MQ's goal was simple: make printing easy. Customers should be able to skip the queue, upload their documents and get their prints delivered. But inquiries still depended on social media. There was no website built to show up in local search, no clear breakdown of services and no structured way to capture a lead.

My Role

I owned the project from start to finish:

  • Planning the website and system with the owner
  • Designing the layouts and user experience
  • Building the front end and back end
  • Setting up the domain, hosting and launch
  • Handling updates, backups and uptime monitoring

The Process

  • Understand the vision. I started with what Michael, the owner, wanted printing with MQ to feel like.
  • Make it visible early. I shared clear layouts so Michael could react to something real.
  • Build in phases. Each phase came with an update, so there were no surprises.
  • Improve along the way. I suggested changes wherever the plan needed help.

The Solution

One system for both sides of the business:

  • For customers: a website built for local search, clear service pages, file upload, online ordering and quote requests, plus an account for tracking orders and reordering.
  • For the team: the MQ Portal, which brings job orders, sales, production and the time clock into one place, with staff pricing, approving and updating orders from one dashboard.

Results & Learnings

The results are showing up on Google. In the latest 28 days, the site earned 114 clicks and 10.7K impressions, and people are finding MQ through searches like “printing services near me” and “printing services cebu”.

It's reaching real customers too. One had messaged more than 15 print shops in a single night, and MQ was the only one that replied. After trying the website and online ordering and showing them to their own boss, they sent MQ this:

“Tell your boss. He/She is way ahead of his/her competitors.”An MQ Prints customer

What I learned: showing clear layouts early made it much easier for a client to say what they really wanted. And for customers, being easy to reach and easy to order from is what stands out.
