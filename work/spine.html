Client: Spine & Orthopaedics Cebu

Connecting patients with orthopaedic specialists across Cebu

Website Development • Healthcare • Responsive Design

Built while at Boombox Marketing for Spine & Orthopaedics Cebu. The website and brand belong to their owners; shown here only as a sample of my work.

Project Overview

Spine & Orthopaedics Cebu is a network of orthopaedic specialists and clinics. I developed a responsive, content-rich website that helps patients find the right doctor and clinic.

The Challenge

The clinic network needed a trustworthy online presence that showcases its specialists, explains conditions and services, makes clinic locations easy to find, and supports patient education through blogs and foundation initiatives.

My Role

I was responsible for the website's development:

  • Building the specialist, conditions and location pages
  • Creating clear paths to book a consultation or send an inquiry
  • Adding the blog and foundation pages
  • Making every layout responsive and accessible

The Process

  • Start with what patients look for. Doctors, conditions and clinic locations came first.
  • Make the next step obvious. Each key page leads to a consultation or inquiry.
  • Support education. Blog and foundation pages give patients useful information beyond the clinic.

The Solution

Specialist profiles

  • Dedicated doctor pages with bios and specialties
  • Clear CTAs to schedule consultations

Conditions & services

  • Informational sections on treatments offered
  • Navigation built for quick access to health info

Clinic locations

  • Affiliated hospitals and clinic addresses
  • “Talk to Us” inquiry paths

Education & outreach

  • Blog and foundation pages
  • Responsive, accessible layouts

Results & Learnings

  • Professional online presence that builds patient trust.
  • Improved accessibility — doctors, services and locations are easy to find.
  • Ongoing educational value through blogs and foundation content.
  • More inquiries through clear contact pathways.

What I learned: on a healthcare website, trust comes from clarity. People need to find the right doctor quickly and know exactly what to do next.
